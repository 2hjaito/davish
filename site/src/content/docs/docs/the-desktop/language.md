The whole interface comes in English, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish (Spain), German, Korean and French: menus, dialogs, help, error messages, the dates and numbers. The first time, Davish follows your system's language; after that, pick one in Settings → General → Language. Davish restarts to switch, and your servers reconnect on their own.
